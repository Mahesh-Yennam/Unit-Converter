# Universal Unit Converter Pro — Gamility Tools

A modern, high-precision, client-side metric and imperial unit conversion engine with live two-way synchronization, all-unit comparative matrices, formula references, and custom precision scaling.

---

## 🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

All unit transformations and numerical calculations execute **entirely within your local web browser**. No numerical inputs, financial values, or conversion activities are ever uploaded, recorded, or shared with external servers or cloud APIs.

---

## ✨ Features

- **8 Comprehensive Measurement Categories**:
  - **Length & Distance**: Millimeters, Centimeters, Meters, Kilometers, Inches, Feet, Yards, Miles, Nautical Miles.
  - **Mass & Weight**: Milligrams, Grams, Kilograms, Metric Tons, Ounces, Pounds, Stones.
  - **Temperature**: Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), Kelvin (K) with scientific offset calibration.
  - **Area**: Square Meters, Square Kilometers, Square Feet, Square Yards, Acres, Hectares.
  - **Volume & Capacity**: Milliliters, Liters, Cubic Meters, Fluid Ounces, Cups, Pints, Quarts, US Gallons, Imperial Gallons.
  - **Speed & Velocity**: Meters/sec (m/s), Kilometers/hour (km/h), Miles/hour (mph), Knots (kn), Feet/sec (ft/s).
  - **Digital Data Storage**: Bits, Bytes, Kilobytes, Megabytes, Gigabytes, Terabytes, Petabytes (decimal $1000$ vs binary $1024$ mode).
  - **Time & Duration**: Milliseconds, Seconds, Minutes, Hours, Days, Weeks, Months, Years.
- **Bi-Directional Synchronous Conversion**:
  - Live two-way inputs: Edit either field to instantly recalculate the counterpart.
  - One-tap Swap button ($\rightleftarrows$) to reverse measurement directions.
- **All-Units Instant Comparison Grid**:
  - Displays the equivalent value across every single unit in the active category at a glance.
- **Precision & Formatting Controls**:
  - Decimal precision selector (Auto, 2, 4, 6, 8 decimals).
  - Scientific notation support for sub-atomic or astronomical figures.
- **Formula & Mathematical Factor Inspector**:
  - Shows the exact conversion formula and standard multiplier equation.
- **Quick Preset Examples**:
  - Single-click loading of popular measurements (e.g. Marathon distance, human body temperature, speed of sound, digital gigabytes).
- **Responsive Dark Design**: Tailored glassmorphism UI styled for desktop, tablet, and mobile displays.
